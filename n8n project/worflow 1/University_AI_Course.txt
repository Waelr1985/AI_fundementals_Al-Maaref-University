DATA SCIENCE ACADEMY
Artificial Intelligence Training Program – 2026

Program Overview

The Data Science Academy offers an 8-month Artificial Intelligence
Training Program designed for beginner and intermediate students.

The program currently has 120 registered students.

Classes are held three times per week:
Monday at 6:00 PM
Wednesday at 6:00 PM
Saturday at 10:00 AM

Each class lasts two hours.

The program is divided into six main modules:

1. Python for Data Science
2. Machine Learning
3. Deep Learning
4. Natural Language Processing
5. Generative AI and Large Language Models
6. AI Agents and RAG

Students complete four practical projects during the program.

The final project requires students to build an AI application
that uses Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).

The RAG module lasts four weeks.

During the RAG module, students learn:
- Document loading
- Text splitting
- Embeddings
- Vector databases
- Semantic search
- AI agents
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Students must achieve at least 70% to successfully complete
the program.

The program coordinator is Dr. Wael Rahhal.

The final project submission deadline is December 18, 2026.
